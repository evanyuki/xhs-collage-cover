# 配图生成稿

## 公园观察手记

`field-hero.png` uses `city-cover.png` as its style reference. Create a near-square, transparent editorial cutout of two full-length young East Asian observers. A woman steps left and bends to inspect a leaf through a magnifying glass. A man springs up to look through binoculars. A leafy branch arches between them beside a sunny yellow field satchel. Use elongated limbs, a tight silhouette and flat 1950s cut-paper gouache in cobalt, navy, red, orange, yellow and cyan. Keep the illustration free of lettering, poster borders, scenery and sticker outlines.
